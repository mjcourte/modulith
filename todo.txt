Wiring IAM, AssetRefs.

iam step 7: cloning

- Who can clone: CloneProject(actor, source, target owner) needs read access to the source and ownership of the target (the actor's own library, or their org). A locked project can only be cloned into its own org.
- What the clone gets: the source's name, a new ID and cloned_from. Shares aren't copied.
- How it runs: the project starts in state cloning, which hides it and grants nothing. Then:
  a. iam creates the project row.
  b. storage runs CopyProjectFiles.
  c. Later, documentmanager copies the document and rewrites its references.
  d. iam marks the project ready.
